The ebook on Amazon

The ebook is available in every Amazon Kindle store. If Amazon shows it as unavailable, the reason is almost always your account, not the book. Each Amazon account buys Kindle books in one store only, and that store is not necessarily the one for the country you are in.

So if the book looks unavailable, open it in the store your account belongs to. You can see which one that is on Amazon under “Account & Lists” (top right), then “Content & Devices” or “Content Library”, then choose the tab “Preferences”, then “Country/Region Settings”.

The ebook is also sold by other retailers. You can find many of them through the universal book link.
